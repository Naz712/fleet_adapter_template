Stair Climber Track Layouts

Four ways to lay out the tracks, each carrying the same person on the same balancing seat arm up and down the same stairs. No split has one hinge in the middle under the seat. The split layouts turn one or both 0.70 m halves into two 0.35 m sections joined by an extra hinge motor, which bends at each edge to keep the tracks on the stairs.
